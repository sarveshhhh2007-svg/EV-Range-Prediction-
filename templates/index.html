Scope Clarification & Boundary Enforcement

The target variable is range_km. The platform predicts the listed laboratory driving range from technical vehicle specifications. It does NOT predict Battery State of Health (SOH), battery degradation over time, remaining battery life, or actual real-world driving range under every adverse weather, topography, or driver condition.

Synthesis & Takeaways

Results & Conclusion

Scientific interpretation of experimental findings, model selection trade-offs, target leakage takeaways, and domain limitations.

Key EDA Findings

Battery capacity exhibits the strongest positive Pearson correlation (r = +0.880) with driving range, followed by top speed (+0.732) and charging power (+0.721). Acceleration time has a strong inverse correlation (-0.712).

Target Leakage Insight

Physical implied range relates mathematically to battery capacity and efficiency. The ablation confirmed that even without efficiency, models maintain R² > 0.95 purely from battery capacity, aerodynamics, and drivetrain dynamics.

Model Recommendation

Linear Regression achieved the lowest 5-fold cross-validation RMSE (17.76 km), slightly outperforming Gradient Boosting (18.85 km) with superior training efficiency and transparent coefficients.

Limitations & Future Scope

  • Ambient Thermal Effects: Dataset reflects laboratory homologation cycles (WLTP/EPA) at 20-23°C; winter HVAC heating draw is unrepresented.
  • Battery Chemistry Variations: All 478 records utilize Lithium-ion chemistries; emerging solid-state or LFP variations cannot be uniquely isolated without chemistry sub-tags.
  • Driver Aggressiveness: Real-world driving patterns (speed, aggressive braking, payload) differ from standardized test cycle profiles.
